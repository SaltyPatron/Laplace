CHESS PROVENANCE AND CONSENSUS
==============================

Purpose
-------

Chess is a modality stress test for the same substrate laws used by language, code, and
models. A game is an ordered trajectory; a move is a witnessed transition; analysis is
calculated testimony; play is a stateful forward pass.

Identity ladder
---------------

- Unicode remains tier 0.
- Pieces, squares, moves, positions, lines, games, players, events, and source records
  are content-addressed entities at their proper compositional altitude.
- Board-state identity is determined by its complete canonical state, not display text.
- Move order and game history live in trajectories.

Recorded testimony
------------------

Game sources record literal headers, players, result, move sequence, clocks when
available, and source provenance. Opening catalogs and tablebase records retain their
own source/context. Player-name trust, game outcome, and opening membership are separate
facts and must not collapse into a generic chess source.

Calculated testimony
--------------------

Engine evaluation, move quality, motifs, missed finishes, time-pressure labels,
tablebase distance calculations, and self-play analysis are versioned analyzer sources.
A calculated move-quality judgment never overwrites or masquerades as the recorded game
result.

Forward pass
------------

Playing a move follows the canonical operation program:

`COMPOSE state → ORIENT task/scope → PROPOSE legal candidates → STEER with witnessed
evidence → SELECT → REALIZE move → WITNESS outcome`

Classical search may serve the proposal stage. It is not the whole Laplace decision.
Consensus, player scope, line continuity, geometry, tablebase facts, motifs, clocks, and
observed outcomes may steer candidates through typed evidence.

Acceptance
----------

- A game round-trips exactly from its trajectory.
- Recorded and calculated chess testimony are source-separable.
- Legal move selection is conditioned on complete state and side to move.
- A played move includes a receipt for proposal, steering, selection, and witness.
- Self-play feedback affects later decisions through ordinary testimony and fold rules.
- Chess-specific caches remain derived from the PostgreSQL system of record.
