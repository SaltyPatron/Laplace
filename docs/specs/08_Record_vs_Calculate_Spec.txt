RECORD VERSUS CALCULATE
=======================

Purpose
-------

Laplace distinguishes what a source states from what an analyzer derives. Both are
testimony, but they have different provenance, invalidation, and trust semantics.

Recorded layer
--------------

A decomposer records only source-observable content and assertions:

- literal content and ordered composition;
- names, identifiers, declared metadata, and source relationships;
- outcomes or labels supplied by the source;
- the source and context responsible for each assertion.

Recording is deterministic transcription. It must not depend on a mutable model,
heuristic threshold, external evaluator, or current consensus ranking.

Calculated layer
----------------

Parsing, classification, projection, correlation, evaluation, move quality, circuit
analysis, inferred relations, and generated outcomes are calculated testimony. Every
calculated witness identifies:

- analyzer identity and version;
- inputs or input scope;
- parameters/recipe;
- output relation and score domain;
- execution outcome and reproducibility receipt.

Calculated testimony may be superseded or recomputed without deleting recorded
evidence. A newer analyzer competes as another witness unless an explicit version
policy scopes it out.

Consensus boundary
------------------

Recorded and calculated sources may fold into a shared typed cell when they make the
same proposition, but source trust and uncertainty remain available. They must not be
collapsed into one indistinguishable source. A calculated proxy must not overwrite the
literal outcome it estimates.

Examples
--------

- A PGN game result is recorded; an engine's blunder score is calculated.
- A tensor value and tensor role are recorded from a checkpoint; a circuit correlation
  with another model is calculated.
- Source code bytes and compiler metadata are recorded; compile/test/lint results are
  calculated and witnessed.
- A user correction is recorded as feedback; its effect on the next ranking is
  calculated by the fold and forward pass.

Acceptance
----------

- Re-ingesting the same source reproduces identities and source testimony.
- Changing an analyzer version changes only its calculated evidence.
- Every derived value can name its analyzer, inputs, and recipe.
- Reads can include, exclude, or compare recorded and calculated source classes.
- Unknown, refuted, and unsupported remain distinct.
