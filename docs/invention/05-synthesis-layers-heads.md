# Substrate model construction: layers, heads, factors, and readout

The normative construction law is in specs 09 and 12. This file describes consumer/model construction from the substrate; it is not the native Laplace cognition ontology and it does not define commercial intelligence tiers. The live substrate remains one knowledge world under explicit knowledge authority and compute envelopes. This note explains the mechanism without claiming that a particular experimental materializer is the finished design.

## Evidence objects

- Canonical entities provide vocabulary/content identity.
- Typed consensus cells provide signed support, uncertainty, witnesses, and source scope.
- Trajectories provide exact order and occurrence.
- Model-circuit factors/projections provide source-scoped transformation evidence.
- Relation bands, tiers, modalities, and operation recipes provide scheduling axes.

## Hidden dimension

Dimension is a recipe/construction result constrained by spectral rank, typed strata,
factor rank, modality requirements, and output codec. It is not copied blindly from a
source model or selected solely for checkpoint compatibility.

## Embedding/input representation

Input representation combines canonical content placement, source-scoped projections,
typed strata, and trajectory/ordinal state. An identity matrix can isolate a lookup
experiment, but `embed = I` is not the general Laplace representation law.

## Heads and operators

A head is a typed operator over evidence, not merely an ordinal bucket. Candidate head
families include relation/circuit factors, angular or trajectory kernels, containment,
typed gates, discourse routes, and modality operators.

For a source circuit, Q/K/V/O and factor slices remain attributable to that source.
Cross-source functional alignment may correlate different ordinals; equal ordinals do
not share identity by default.

## Layers and schedules

For an exported conventional model, target layers are consumer slots populated by a declared recipe. Native Laplace processing layers/rounds are instead routed folds over typed planes/operators under the canonical forward program. Do not infer a fixed transformer layer stack from the existence of an export recipe. Every native emitted act updates state before the next coupling/frontier round.

## Output/readout

The readout selects typed completion candidates from standing evidence and active state.
Attested continuation distributions, circuit testimony, relation constraints, and
realization policy may contribute. A closed-form log-odds lookup is a useful floor, not
proof of exact contextual completion or general conversation.

Knowledge-package grants are not model-source ablations. Source A/B/A+B is a diagnostic/export scope over authorized evidence; it is not a cheaper product's reduced world.

## Heterogeneous pooled construction

Sources deposit into the canonical substrate; the fold precedes construction and
selection. A pooled construction is not tensor arithmetic, concatenated vocabularies,
runtime routing among original models, or N answers followed by a judge.

## Acceptance

- Source circuits and all materialized slots retain receipts.
- Layer/head schedules derive from declared typed evidence/recipe axes.
- Source A, source B, and pooled A+B are reproducible scopes.
- Cross-architecture sources require no ordinal/dimension/tokenizer equality.
- External exports load and pass held-out semantic/source-ablation tests.
