SUBSTRATE-NATIVE MODEL SYNTHESIS
===============================

Thesis
------

Laplace constructs and executes model behavior from witnessed substrate state. It does
not require stochastic gradient training as its native learning mechanism. Content,
ordered physicality, typed relations, source testimony, uncertainty, geometry, and
observed outcomes are the model state.

The system may ingest conventional checkpoints, but a checkpoint enters as a source of
auditable testimony. The checkpoint is not the permanent runtime authority and its
original architecture does not constrain the architecture of another source.

Universal floor and convergence
-------------------------------

Unicode/DUCET supply the text floor. Canonical entity identity lets equivalent content
from incompatible tokenizers and model families converge without string-proximity or
ordinal-slot identity. S³/Super-Fibonacci placement, Hilbert locality, trajectories,
containment, tiers, and typed relation bands provide deterministic physical execution
axes. Spatial proximity contributes evidence; it is not semantic truth by itself.

Checkpoint witnessing
----------------------

Model ingestion records source-scoped evidence for:

- tensor identity, dtype, shape, slice, and architecture role;
- layer, head, expert, router, embedding, normalization, convolution, and output planes;
- token/entity surfaces touched by a circuit;
- ranked circuit testimony trajectories;
- factor/projection physicalities and exact tensor-slice provenance;
- source, recipe, analyzer version, and observed behavioral outcomes.

A structural coordinate such as `(plane, layer ordinal, head ordinal)` is a comparison
address, not a declaration that two sources contain the same functional circuit.
Functional alignment is calculated from token testimony, factors, trajectories,
geometry, relation behavior, salience, and observed outcomes while retaining both
source circuits.

Pooled heterogeneous-model consensus
------------------------------------

Multiple ingested models participate as witnesses in one shared substrate. They do not
generate N answers for a judge, vote, or concatenation step. They do not require tensor
arithmetic, equal layer counts, compatible hidden sizes, common tokenizer vocabularies,
or a shared modality.

The standing fold occurs before selection. One canonical forward pass consumes the
pooled evidence and emits one answer. Source-scoped A-only, B-only, and A+B execution is
an inspection and ablation facility, not post-hoc adjudication.

Source disagreements remain inspectable. Consensus does not erase dissent, uncertainty,
or provenance. Correlations may be deposited as versioned calculated testimony; they do
not rewrite exact recorded circuit evidence.

Construction and export
-----------------------

Export is a deterministic materialization of a declared substrate program:

- Unicode/ISO plus one model source produces that source's clean, witnessed construction.
- Multiple sources produce the pooled consensus construction.
- Layers, heads, experts, dimensions, operators, and schedules derive from declared
  recipe, relation bands, tiers, content coverage, trajectories, and evidence—not a
  hardcoded Llama template.
- Every emitted tensor/slot has a receipt to source cells and calculations.

GGUF and SafeTensors are codecs/validation targets, not the native ontology. A file on
disk is not success: the artifact must load in the declared external runtime and pass
held-out semantic, source-ablation, and provenance checks.

FAITHFUL constraint
-------------------

Closed-form lookup experiments demonstrate that substrate-derived weights can produce
non-empty behavior without optimizer artifacts. They do not establish faithful model
construction merely because `embed = I` or a matrix lookup emits plausible neighbors.
The target is exact, typed, contextual selection—e.g. a constrained proposition selects
its supported completion—validated end to end rather than by isolated token output.

Acceptance
----------

- Incompatible causal, encoder/embedding, reranker/MoE, and multimodal sources coexist.
- `entity → source circuits`, `coordinate → source differences`, and cross-ordinal
  functional correlation are typed, indexed operations with receipts.
- Pooled execution emits one result without running an external answer-writing model.
- Source ablation changes evidence in explainable ways.
- Deterministic exports load and preserve scoped behavior.
- No GPU is required by the substrate execution contract.
