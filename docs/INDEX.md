## Important supersession notes

`INVENTION_PRESERVATION_2026-09-02.md` is an intermediate historical reconciliation memo. It no longer corrects or outranks `INVENTION.md`; its durable findings have been incorporated into the canonical documents.

Dated completion/execution plans under `docs/plan/` may describe a particular campaign. They do not become a permanent global implementation order merely because this index links to them. Current inventor instruction and the authority order in `AGENTS.md` decide active scope/order.

Likewise, issue ledgers/status reports describe implementation work and evidence. They may not narrow the invention around a partial implementation, old model, old benchmark, MVP, fallback or convenient search operator.

## Design specifications

Binding current specifications include the substrate invariants, engineering rules, record-vs-calculate law, synthesis/chess/provenance/perfcache contracts and forward/operation specs identified in `AGENTS.md`.

The specs are read under `INVENTION.md`; they do not override a later explicit inventor correction.
